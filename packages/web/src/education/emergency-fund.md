# Emergency Fund

An emergency fund is cash set aside for unexpected expenses. It is the first line of defense before you touch investments or go into debt.

## How Much Should You Save?

| Situation | Recommended Fund |
|-----------|------------------|
| Single, stable job, low obligations | 3 months of expenses |
| Family with one income | 6–9 months of expenses |
| Self-employed or irregular income | 9–12 months of expenses |

## Where to Keep It

The emergency fund should be:

- **Liquid** — accessible within a day or two.
- **Safe** — not exposed to market risk.
- **Separate** — kept in a different account from daily spending.

A high-yield savings account or money-market account is usually the best home for this money.

## When to Use It

Use the fund for genuine emergencies only:

- Job loss
- Medical or dental emergencies
- Major car or home repairs
- Family emergencies

Routine expenses, sales, or wants are not emergencies. When you do use the fund, make a plan to refill it.
